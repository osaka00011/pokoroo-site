Pokoroo

Hide money from yourself

Pokoroo is a budgeting app for iPhone. When you save for a goal, the money goes into Pokoroo's pouch and stops counting as money you can spend. The number you see is what you can really spend right now.

Using pouch money for the thing you saved for is one tap. Taking it back out for something else takes a reason and a long press, so you think twice. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no ads, no tracking.
