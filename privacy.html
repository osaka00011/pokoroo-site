Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

The short version

  • Everything you enter in Pokoroo stays on your iPhone.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Nothing is sent to us. We never see your data.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information.
  • Face ID is handled by iOS. We never see it.
  • Exports and backups happen only when you start them, and you choose where they go.
  • Deleting the app deletes its data.

This policy explains how the Pokoroo iPhone app (“Pokoroo”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. In short: the app is built so that we never have it.

What Pokoroo stores, and where

Pokoroo stores what you enter: your currency, your starting balance, income and expenses with their notes and categories, your pouches (savings goals) and the money moved in and out of them, your recurring income and bills, and the optional monthly spending estimate you give during setup.

It also keeps a few settings on your device, such as whether the lock is on, whether widgets hide amounts, and how many days you've opened the app. That last one is used only to decide when to show Apple's standard App Store review request.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, which its widgets can read. Suggestions, goal estimates and milestones are calculated on your iPhone from your own records. Nothing is sent to a server, because Pokoroo has no server.

What we collect

Nothing. Pokoroo has:

  • no account or sign-in;
  • no analytics, usage statistics or crash reporting;
  • no ads and no advertising identifiers;
  • no tracking, and no third-party code that tracks you;
  • no connections of its own to the internet. The only network activity is the App Store checking your purchase, which iOS handles (see below).

Nothing you enter in Pokoroo is ever sent to us. We can't see your money, your goals or your notes.

Purchases

Pokoroo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your name, email address, payment details or any other personal information from your purchase.

To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app asks Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone whether your Apple Account owns it. Nothing about your money is sent with that check.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

Pokoroo has an optional lock. When it's on, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode, and only tells Pokoroo whether unlocking succeeded. Pokoroo never receives, sees or stores your Face ID data, fingerprints or passcode.

Whether or not the lock is on, Pokoroo hides your amounts in the app switcher.

Widgets

Pokoroo's widgets read the same data on your iPhone. They don't send it anywhere. You can turn on Hide amounts in widgets in Settings, and iOS hides widget amounts on a locked iPhone.

Exports and backups

Pokoroo can make a backup file, a spreadsheet (CSV) of your transactions, or a PDF statement. These are made on your iPhone, and only when you start them from Settings. Pokoroo hands the file to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes: for example, the Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or email.

Once you save or share a file, it's in your hands and in the hands of the service you chose. Its own privacy policy applies there. These files aren't encrypted, so anyone you share one with can read it. Pokoroo warns you about this before you export.

Restoring reads only a backup file that you pick.

iPhone backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include Pokoroo's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. You and Apple manage those backups. We have no access to them.

Deleting your data

Deleting Pokoroo from your iPhone deletes all of its data, including what its widgets show. Files you exported yourself, and iPhone backups made by iOS, aren't affected. Delete those where you saved them if you no longer want them.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete, correct or send you, and we can't recover data for you.

Children

Pokoroo doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Pokoroo changes how your data is handled, for example by adding a sync feature, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
