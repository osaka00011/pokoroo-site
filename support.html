Questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Pokoroo, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. You'll also find it on the Pokoroo Pro screen. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought Pro with, and that you're online. Pro is a one-time purchase, so you never pay for it twice.

How do backup and restore work?

To back up: go to Settings and tap Back up everything. Pokoroo makes one file with all your transactions, pouches and recurring entries, and opens the share sheet so you can save it where you like, for example in iCloud Drive or on your computer. Backups are free.

To restore: go to Settings, tap Restore from backup and pick the file. Pokoroo checks the whole file first. If it can't be used, the app tells you why and changes nothing. If it can, you'll be asked to confirm, because restoring replaces everything currently in Pokoroo, and that can't be undone.

A backup can only be restored into Pokoroo set to the same currency. Backup files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

The surest way is a Pokoroo backup:

  1. On your old iPhone, go to Settings, tap Back up everything, and save the file to iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it to your new iPhone.
  2. On your new iPhone, install Pokoroo and set it up with the same currency as before. Anything you enter during setup will be replaced.
  3. Go to Settings, tap Restore from backup, pick the file and confirm.
  4. If you have Pro, tap Restore purchases.

If you move everything to your new iPhone with Apple's transfer or an iPhone backup, Pokoroo's data may come along with it. Make a Pokoroo backup first anyway, just in case.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Pokoroo app: touch and hold its icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. This deletes all of Pokoroo's data from your iPhone, including what its widgets show.

Backup files, spreadsheets or statements you exported aren't touched. Delete them where you saved them. Your iPhone's own backups may also hold a copy until they're replaced.

We have nothing to delete on our side, because your data never reaches us.

Can you recover my data?

No, and we're sorry about that. Pokoroo keeps your data only on your iPhone, and never sends it to us, so we never have a copy. If the app is deleted or your iPhone is lost, only a backup can bring your data back. Back up from time to time, and keep the file somewhere other than your iPhone.
